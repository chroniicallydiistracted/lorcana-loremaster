# Archetype Library

**Scope:** Reusable method; select the actual format, date, rules and card pool. Strategic guidance is not a measured current metagame.

For current Core packages and copy ranges, use the [October 4 profiles](current-archetypes.md)
and [deck-review workflow](deck-review-workflow.md). These categories are general
analysis templates, not a current tier list.

## Aggro

**Goal:** Reach 20 lore before the opponent stabilizes.

**Needs:** High density of early characters, efficient lore, protection or reach, low opportunity cost for inking.

**Common failure:** Trading too much and abandoning the race; overextending into mass interaction; hands with pressure but no ink plan.

## Tempo

**Goal:** Develop threats while using cheap interaction to delay opposing development.

**Needs:** Efficient bodies, bounce/exert/damage, evasive pressure, cards that preserve initiative.

**Common failure:** Using interaction without a clock; spending premium answers on low-impact cards; falling behind in cards.

## Midrange

**Goal:** Win board combat with efficient threats and flexible answers, then convert control into lore.

**Needs:** Strong curve, favorable challenge math, resilient value, enough draw to avoid stalling.

**Common failure:** Drawing the wrong half; being slower than control and less focused than aggro.

## Control

**Goal:** Extend the game, trade favorably, and win after opposing threats are exhausted.

**Needs:** Broad answers, card advantage, healing or stabilization, inevitability, a plan for evasive and non-character permanents.

**Common failure:** Too many reactive cards; no clean closing plan; losing to early lore before answers become efficient.

## Ramp

**Goal:** Increase ink faster than normal and deploy high-impact cards ahead of schedule.

**Needs:** Reliable ramp, sufficient inkable cards, stabilizers, worthwhile top end, protection from tempo loss.

**Common failure:** Drawing payoffs without ramp or ramp without payoffs; spending early turns without affecting the board.

## Combo / Engine

**Goal:** Assemble interactions whose combined output exceeds normal card efficiency.

**Needs:** Redundant pieces, tutors/selection, protection, fallback plan, clear combo timing.

**Common failure:** Pieces are individually weak; the opponent disrupts a single bottleneck; the engine produces value but not a win.

## Evasive

**Goal:** Create a race opponents cannot answer efficiently through normal challenges.

**Needs:** Evasive density, efficient lore, protection, answers for opposing Evasive cards and races.

**Common failure:** Low stats into direct removal; inability to stabilize; losing the mirror to better curve or interaction.

## Songs

**Goal:** Convert character development into ink savings by singing impactful actions.

**Needs:** Appropriate singer curve, useful songs at every stage, enough characters, protection from losing singers before use.

**Common failure:** Hands with songs and no singers; exerting essential questers at the wrong time; high-value songs that are poor when paid normally.

## Items

**Goal:** Build persistent value from items and item-matters effects.

**Needs:** Correct item density, payoffs that survive interaction, draw, tempo tools, plan against item removal.

**Common failure:** Many low-impact pieces; board loss while assembling; engine dependent on one vulnerable card.

## Locations

**Goal:** Accumulate passive lore or gain positional advantages that force awkward challenges.

**Needs:** Locations that justify their tempo cost, movement synergies, protection, favorable challenge math.

**Common failure:** Locations that are easy to remove; spending too much ink moving characters; insufficient character board.

## Discard

**Goal:** Reduce opposing options, then exploit an empty or constrained hand.

**Needs:** Timely discard, pressure, repeatable value, ways to avoid weak late topdecks.

**Common failure:** Discard drawn after the opponent empties their hand; not affecting the board; no payoff for the resource advantage.

## Classification tribal

**Goal:** Use a shared classification such as Hero, Villain, Princess, Floodborn, Monster, Red Panda, or another supported group to unlock rate bonuses.

**Needs:** Sufficient classification density, payoffs worth the restriction, overlap with naturally strong cards.

**Common failure:** Including weak members only to reach density; payoffs that require unrealistic board states.
