# Archetype evidence — October 4, 2026

Eight exact Core lists from three recent events support these six representative profiles. Event dates, format and roster sizes are corroborated by official Play Hub pages. Lists and finishes are secondary reports; field sizes disagree in places and final official standings were not visible. This purposeful sample does not support global metashare or matchup win rates.

Copy ranges are observations. Packages, engines, flex choices, weaknesses and coaching are strategic inference. A one-list profile cannot establish a mandatory core. Hyperia cards are theorycrafting until the verified October 16 US prerelease; refresh policy for later dates.

## Amber/Emerald Elinor

Develop a broad character board, convert exerted bodies into Elinor value, and protect profitable quests with bodyguards, bounce and Shift.

- [Kezy, 2026-09-27, reported #1](https://lorcanahunter.com/tournament/16598/spielhohle-regensburg-setmeisterschaft-angriff-der-ranke/player/166030/deck); [official event](https://tcg.ravensburgerplay.com/events/832430).

| cost savings | Pluto - Friendly Pooch; Grandmother Willow - Ancient Advisor; Aurora - Holding Court | Discounts improve deployment but do not add permanent ink; protect enough inkable cards. |
| character access | Mike Wazowski - Heroic Climber; Nani - Stage Manager; Pocahontas - Guiding the Tribe | An all-character deck improves Mike reveals; Nani and Pocahontas reward cheap-character density. |
| engine and protection | Elinor - Renowned Diplomat; Dale - Ready for His Shot; David - Protective Snowboarder; Pocahontas - Peacekeeper | Elinor rewards exerting; Dale uses current willpower for challenge damage; shifted Peacekeeper conditionally prevents challenges. |
| interaction | Ursula - Deceiver; Rapunzel - Tower Defender | Disrupt songs or bounce a chosen character; discarding for Rapunzel has a resource cost. |

Flex choices: Observed Squeaks versus Mickey Amber Champion packages change evasion, bounce density and Dale challenge math. Saved Moluk slots such as Mowgli and Mickey Expedition Leader remain defensible alternatives; current samples do not prove a strict upgrade.

Weaknesses: Broad removal can punish an exerted board. Mike also provides access to opponents; bodyguards do not stop non-challenge interaction.

Mulligan: Prioritize an inkable one-cost character and a coherent two-cost follow-up. Keep discounts alongside actual deployment and ink; trim redundant expensive pieces. Keep Ursula more often against known song engines.

Sequencing: Resolve character discounts in the required order, develop cheap bodies before Nani/Elinor, and check Peacekeeper before any challenge. Consider unused Pluto exertion only when its cost and the Elinor threshold justify it.

## Emerald/Sapphire Mickey & Minnie

Assemble both named bases for a free Duo Shift, convert character value into resilient pressure, and use interaction to preserve a questing window.

- [Narfdre, 2026-10-03, reported #13](https://lorcanahunter.com/tournament/16854/game-grid-lehi-game-grid-open-ccq/player/168088/deck?lf_set=overall&lf_sort=date_desc&lf_tournamentId=16854); [official event](https://tcg.ravensburgerplay.com/events/1016280).

| Package | Cards | Purpose |
|---|---|---|
| Mickey bases | Mickey Mouse - Bob Cratchit; Mickey Mouse - Detective | Mickey access is a separate requirement from Minnie access; Detective provides permanent ink. |
| Minnie bases | Minnie Mouse - Curious Adventurer; Minnie Mouse - Practical Traveler; Minnie Mouse - Spinning Skater | Cheap Minnie versions provide pressure and the second distinct Duo Shift base. |
| Duo payoff | Mickey Mouse & Minnie Mouse - Adventuring Duo | The zero Shift payment still requires both bases and carries their relevant state; printed cost is not the play curve. |
| card flow | Tod - Clever Fox; Strike A Good Match; Donald Duck - Perfect Gentleman; Milo Thatch - Getting His Hands Dirty | Separate filtering and conditional draw from net resources; Donald also offers opponents cards. |
| interaction | Chernabog - Unnatural Force; Milo Thatch - Getting His Hands Dirty; Hades - Infernal Schemer; Under the Sea | Compare chosen removal with mass bottom-decking. Chernabog and Hades may give opponents resources. |
| resilient pressure | Hades - Meticulous Schemer; Winifred - Exasperated Elephant; Scuttle - Birdbrained | Ward constrains choosing, not challenges or non-choosing mass effects. |

Flex choices: The current sample uses fewer vanilla Ward bodies and a broader removal suite than the saved Baha list. Prince Phillip at nine printed ink lacks a base in this sample; do not treat its Shift as generally available.

Weaknesses: Removing either base interrupts the early Duo line. Large printed costs and conditional draw can produce slow hands despite high inkability.

Mulligan: Keep distinct Mickey and Minnie bases plus usable ink. Keep the Duo when both bases and sequencing are credible; return duplicate expensive threats. Detective matters when the hand needs a route to the late game.

Sequencing: Choose which versions to expose, track whether both bases are dry and their damage, and calculate the cost of converting two bodies into one. Do not assume Ward prevents Under the Sea.

## Amethyst/Emerald Retro

Use card flow and Retro Evolution Device to convert small bodies into increasingly powerful on-play interaction and closing threats.

- [[Ink Pass] LegendGary, 2026-10-03, reported #1](https://lorcanahunter.com/tournament/16854/game-grid-lehi-game-grid-open-ccq/player/168118/deck); [official event](https://tcg.ravensburgerplay.com/events/1016280).

| Package | Cards | Purpose |
|---|---|---|
| early bodies | Aladdin - Doing His Part; Aladdin - Prince Ali; Lyle Tiberius Rourke - Adventurer for Hire | Supply deployable bodies and Aladdin bases rather than relying on expensive opening hands. |
| card flow | Tod - Clever Fox; Aladdin & Genie - Mischievous Pals; Hades - Looking for a Deal; Junior Woodchuck Guidebook | Filtering, hand replacement and item value support repeated threats; evaluate their exact costs. |
| upgrade engine | Retro Evolution Device | The item exerts, costs one ink and banishes your character; it plays a replacement up to two printed cost higher. It is not a Shift and the new body normally dries. |
| removal and finishers | Milo Thatch - Getting His Hands Dirty; Prince Phillip - Vanquisher of Foes; Malicious, Mean, and Scary; Under the Sea | Damage enables Prince Phillip; choose mass removal according to strength, damage and timing. |

Flex choices: The observed champion uses four Device and three Under the Sea; this single list does not establish mandatory copy counts.

Weaknesses: Item removal or insufficient sacrificial bodies interrupts upgrade sequences. Banishing bodies can cost pressure; mass removal and tempo bounce can delay recovery.

Mulligan: Keep early bodies with an ink plan; keep Device when its deployment and conversion costs are realistic. Return redundant expensive finishers unless hand replacement and sequencing justify them.

Sequencing: Plan printed-cost upgrade steps and the one-ink activation before sacrificing. Save an appropriate damage enabler for Prince Phillip rather than assuming every opposing character is damaged.

## Amber/Emerald Songs and recursion

Develop singers, turn song payments into tempo and card flow, then recur high-impact characters to control the board and close.

- [ItsNotSlowPlay, 2026-10-03, reported #2](https://lorcanahunter.com/tournament/16854/game-grid-lehi-game-grid-open-ccq/player/168128/deck?lf_set=overall&lf_sort=date_desc&lf_tournamentId=16854); [official event](https://tcg.ravensburgerplay.com/events/1016280).

| Package | Cards | Purpose |
|---|---|---|
| singers | Angel - Siren Singer; Ursula - Vanessa; Ariel - Ethereal Voice | Printed cost and Singer value differ; a newly played singer normally cannot exert to sing. |
| filtering and tempo songs | Strike A Good Match; Akood et Emuti; Malicious, Mean, and Scary | Choose singing versus questing according to the board; discounts do not guarantee a character in hand. |
| recursion | Circle of Life; Meilin Lee - Losing Control | Use an actual discard target and the exact card timing rather than assuming a live engine in every opening. |
| large interaction | Milo Thatch - Getting His Hands Dirty; Prince Phillip - Vanquisher of Foes; Under the Sea | Mass answers have different conditions and costs; singing removes potential questers for the turn. |

Flex choices: The observed runner-up uses eight-cost Sing Together songs; verify the actual singer totals and available dry characters.

Weaknesses: Singer removal delays action deployment. Song-heavy hands without characters or discard targets can be inefficient.

Mulligan: Keep a singer and useful follow-up rather than multiple situational songs. On the draw Angel has a card-specific first-turn discount. Adapt keeps to the known opponent, not an assumed matchup percentage.

Sequencing: Decide which dry singers can be spent without losing the race. Sequence discard targets before recursion and damage before Prince Phillip.

## Emerald/Steel character tempo

Develop early characters, use damage and challenge interaction to preserve initiative, and convert Shift/Evasive threats into lore.

| Package | Cards | Purpose |
|---|---|---|

| Shift and evasion | Tod & Copper - Best of Friends; Rapunzel & Flynn Rider - Unlikely Pair; Mushu - Stealthy Dragon | Lower-cost deployment and evasion can preserve tempo, but check real bases and dry status. |
| engines | Elinor - Renowned Diplomat; Vixey - Forest Friend; Angel - Experiment 624 | Assess these exact abilities within the body/damage plan, not only the color-pair label. |

Flex choices: The single selected list finished outside the top cut; inclusion supplies a representative plan, not proof of comparative strength.

Weaknesses: Losing cheap bodies weakens damage scaling and Shift access. Broad removal can exhaust pressure before the deck closes.

Mulligan: Keep early development with a relevant answer. Keep Shift payoffs alongside bases and sufficient ink; avoid a hand dominated by conditional interaction.

Sequencing: Count current characters for damage effects and choose challenge trades that preserve the clock. Do not infer a favorable matchup from the provider archetype label.
