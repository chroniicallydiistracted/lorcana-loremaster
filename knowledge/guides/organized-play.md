# Organized play and Lore Guide assistance

Identify event name, region, date, format, level and official term sheet. Check the
current Resources hub and organizer's actual listing. Do not assume every Challenge,
CCQ or Set Championship uses the same format, rounds, cutoff or prizes.

## Preparation and procedure

Validate the exact deck and registration sheet, eligible printings, accessories,
language requirements, match structure, time limits and information rules. Cite the
specific Tournament Rules section and any event override. Distinguish general
recommendations from binding event requirements.

During a dispute preserve the position, call a Lore Guide and state observable events
in order. The Lead Lore Guide is the event's final arbiter. Explain applicable policy
and possible correction categories without inventing a penalty or promising a rewind.
Use the Play Correction Guidelines and current event level for remedies. Takebacks
require the official conditions and Lore Guide approval; they are not an entitlement.

For coaching and notes, verify device/information and outside-assistance policy.
Do not supply live outside advice to an active sanctioned match where prohibited;
offer allowed preparation, hypothetical analysis or post-match review instead.

## Find and verify an event

The [Ravensburger Play Hub](https://www.ravensburgerplay.com/) links to Lorcana event
discovery and Carde.io organizer accounts. Carde.io's
[event workflow](https://support.carde.io/en/articles/14466191-running-an-event-on-carde-io)
describes player result submission and organizers publishing standings. Visibility
depends on what an organizer actually publishes. Registration, listing, pairings,
Swiss standings and the completed elimination bracket are distinct records.

Open the exact listing and record its native event ID, timezone and format. Check
whether a displayed placement is Swiss rank or final finish, whether drops/byes
affect the field count, and whether a list was submitted before play or transcribed
afterwards. Reconcile discovery sites against the upstream event. An event called a
"Championship" still needs its own date, level and terms verified.

Independent Lore Guide training can support study. The
[Lore Guide Institute](https://institute.lorcanajudges.com/) describes its classes as
free community education; its badges are not official certifications. For an event
staffing requirement, use the organizer's terms and official qualification evidence.
