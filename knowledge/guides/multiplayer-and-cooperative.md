# Multiplayer, Coconut and cooperative products

**Checked:** 2026-10-04. Identify the exact mode, player count, teammates, product,
scenario, difficulty and agreed custom rules before answering.

## Ordinary multiplayer

CR 9 adds multiplayer procedures, including turn progression to the left and ordered
participation when multiple players act. It also provides simultaneous-turn procedure
for modes that use it. During simultaneous Main phases, one player completes a turn
action before another begins; the team chooses its shared bag order under CR 9.3.
This is not permission to share cards or ink unless the exact mode says so.

Identify the meaning of player, opponent, teammate, you and each in the actual card
text. Count all affected players. Ordinary rules still apply unless the selected mode
changes them. CR 9.1 explicitly does not provide the complete rules for every team
game; consult the mode's instructions.

Strategically, consider several opponents' combined clock, broad effects, opportunity
cost of spending removal on one player, and whether intervention gives another player
the win. Table expectations about politics and agreements are preferences, not invented
tournament policy. Separate competitive optimization from a group's desired experience.

## Official Coconut beta

The currently linked official beta rules specify:

- Choose a Coconut support card in the app, linked to a particular ordinary Lorcana card.
- Choose up to three inks, including the support card's ink.
- Build at least 60 cards: up to four copies of the linked ordinary card, at most
  one copy of each other card.
- Use normal multiplayer rules with a victory threshold of at least 25 lore.
- Three or four players are recommended.

Read the exact support card image for its special abilities. The API provides 27
Coconut records as image/name metadata; `data/derived/coconut.json` retains them.
They are not ordinary player-card identities, and their abilities are not transcribed
or automatically validated here. The short beta rules do not establish every release,
ban or construction-exception edge case. Verify those before claiming event legality.

## Illumineer's Quest

Deep Trouble, Palace Heist and The Great Hunny Rescue are distinct cooperative
products. Retrieve the chosen product's complete rules/scenario booklet before
resolving setup, boss actions, challenge targets, player elimination, special resources,
difficulty, deck restrictions or victory. The catalog's encounter-card text alone is
not a complete boss ruleset. Keep encounter cards separate from ordinary player decks.

The Great Hunny Rescue US product page lists October 2, 2026 availability. Its product
page is cached; the full scenario booklet is not yet part of this workspace. Existing
Quest records in the API therefore do not imply complete cooperative adjudication coverage.

For strategic help, assign complementary team roles, compare answers to the actual
encounter's threats, and coordinate action order within the product's permitted rules.
Any custom difficulty, campaign or Game Master roleplay must be labeled house-rule material.
