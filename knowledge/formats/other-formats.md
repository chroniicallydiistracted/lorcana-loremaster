# Preconstructed, Pack Rush and custom formats

**Checked:** 2026-10-04. Select the actual mode before borrowing ordinary rules.

## Preconstructed tournaments

TR 1.3.2.1 describes supplied single-player decks and organizer-authorized changes
from supplied sealed product. The resulting deck is at least 60 cards, may include
more than two inks under Limited rules, and is an Entry-level tournament format.
The organizer decides which permitted products can change the deck; do not assume
cards from a personal collection may be added.

## Pack Rush

The official quick rules and CR 10.2 define a faster two-pack Limited game:

- Shuffle the game cards from two unopened packs; no ink restriction.
- Start with five cards in hand and two ready ink supplied by marketing/puzzle cards.
- The winner reaches at least 15 lore.
- CR 10.2.5 directs a player who runs out of deck cards to shuffle the discard as their new deck.

Use normal game rules for the remainder, including the applicable opening-hand
alteration and starting-player procedures. Marketing cards cannot be moved out of the inkwell or chosen by effects under CR
10.2.2.1–10.2.2.2. The older quick sheet frames reshuffling as a draw with an empty
deck; use the current CR 10.2.5 for exact timing rather than silently combining the
two wordings. The general TR description assumes
24 game cards; CR 10.2 defines the deck by actual cards in the two packs. Verify
product composition rather than extrapolating to future collector boosters.

## Coconut beta

This is an official thematic multiplayer beta with its own construction and
25-lore victory threshold. See `knowledge/guides/multiplayer-and-cooperative.md`.
It is not ordinary Infinity construction and its support cards are separate.

## Historical and house-rule formats

For a historical event, retrieve its contemporary rules, rotation, ban and release
policy. Modern rules and current API fields do not establish historical eligibility.
The old CSV snapshots in `archive/knowledge-2026-07/` are secondary leads with
superseded assumptions, not a complete historical official record.

For a custom format record deck minimum, inks, copies, eligible products, bans,
victory threshold, turn order, setup, resource exceptions and multiplayer/team
handling. Label proposed rules as house rules. Confirm the agreed variant before
checking a list or adjudicating an unusual interaction.
