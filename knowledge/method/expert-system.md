# Lorcana analysis and decision system

**Revision:** 2026-10-04. This document governs reasoning, not official facts.

## Establish the actual task

Determine whether the user wants an explanation, a legal ruling, a recommendation,
a list, an exact calculation, a purchase comparison, coaching, or story research.
Identify mode, date, region, player perspective, audience, desired competitiveness,
collection/budget and relevant event/product. Infer routine defaults and explain
material assumptions. Ask only for missing information that changes the answer.

## Build an evidence chain

1. Route through `knowledge/INDEX.md` and select applicable official sources.
2. Check source freshness and effective dates. A local snapshot is not a live check.
3. Retrieve exact identities/printings; review text conflicts and special card categories.
   Use `knowledge.py search` for actual rule sections/pages and `lorcana.py show`
   for the dated card resolution. Reviewed FAQ answers are a starting reference;
   they do not adjudicate a different question or an unspecified interaction.
4. Separate official facts, calculations, empirical observations and inference.
5. Identify which missing evidence would change the conclusion and retrieve it.

For lore use narrative authority; for collecting use printing and market authority;
for competitive play use rules plus same-format results. Sources from one area do
not automatically prove claims in another.

## Reason within the format

For Constructed, articulate a route to the winning lore threshold, resource plan,
pressure/recovery/closing plan and interaction matrix. Count curve, inkability,
singer/song, Shift base/payoff, classification and engine density. Distinguish printed
cost from actual turn plans and include ink-drop resources separately.

For Limited, verify the personal pool before ranking combinations. Compare at least
plausible alternative builds/picks by reliable curve, floor, inkability, character
count, removal, evasion and available synergy. Every recommended quantity must exist
in the pool. Reusable archetypes are hypotheses, not current tier claims.

For multiplayer/cooperative play, use the exact mode's players, targets, turns,
victory threshold, resource rules and product-specific deck restrictions. Do not
transfer two-player or Core assumptions automatically.

For gameplay, validate actions then compare legal candidate lines within the player's
information. Track time to win, opponent's clock, immediate stabilization, future
board/resources and comeback exposure. See `strategy/gameplay.md`.

## Quantify what is measurable

Use exact sampling calculations for draw-only questions. Apply the actual mulligan
procedure to replacement calculations. For gameplay-dependent effects use explicit
enumeration or a validated simulation, with assumptions and uncertainty. A goldfish
cannot establish opponent matchup win rate. Never invent a tool run or test result.

For empirical rates distinguish games, matches, participants, published top cuts,
event winners and registered decks. Report sample size and missingness. Archetype
popularity is evidence about opponents, not proof of optimization.

## Produce a useful decision

Lead with the answer or recommended line. Give exact changes to an existing list,
balanced additions/cuts, relevant deterministic metrics and why the change helps.
Use compact reasoning appropriate to the user's level. Cite consequential official
claims; give observation dates for current meta/prices and name unverified assumptions.
Offer a specific fallback or test when multiple choices remain defensible.

The purpose is reliable breadth: confidently answer what the evidence supports and
identify the source needed for the remainder. Do not promise exhaustive knowledge
because a base-card catalog is complete.
