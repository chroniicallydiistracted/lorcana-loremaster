## Matchup coaching

All matchup guidance below is strategic inference; adapt to the actual opponent's list and public board.

### Amber/Emerald Elinor

Keep/return: Keep an inkable one-drop and a two-cost follow-up; retain Dale with a board-building hand. Return duplicate late payoffs and excessive uninkable discounts without ink support.

Ink plan: Protect one Elinor and the cheap development needed to reach three exerted characters. Ink surplus late cards only after comparing the opponent’s clock.

Sequence and priorities: Dale and David change challenge trades. Compare trading into their engine with racing; time shifted Peacekeeper before any of your challenges and keep a route to repeat Elinor triggers.

Observed opposing lists: [Kezy, 2026-09-27](https://lorcanahunter.com/tournament/16598/spielhohle-regensburg-setmeisterschaft-angriff-der-ranke/player/166030/deck); [BZ, 2026-10-03](https://lorcanahunter.com/tournament/16854/game-grid-lehi-game-grid-open-ccq/player/168094/deck?lf_set=overall&lf_sort=date_desc&lf_tournamentId=16854).

### Emerald/Sapphire Mickey & Minnie

Keep/return: Prioritize a cheap curve; keep Ursula only when a relevant song is expected, rather than assuming every list is song-heavy.

Ink plan: Keep a coherent Elinor or Dale line; redundant fours can fund early deployment. Preserve cheap bodies rather than retaining every payoff.

Sequence and priorities: Interrupt a known Shift base when a legal profitable challenge exists. Ward does not prevent challenges. A Duo banish replacement creates ink; evaluate the resulting board and next turn instead of calling banishment a clean removal.
